Notes and definitions

Main site covered. A main site is covered when the main site itself is trained, or when it is not trained but at least one alternate site assigned to it is. Percent coverage = covered main sites divided by total main sites.

Alternate sites. Across all regions 171 alternate sites are listed and 139 are trained. 3 trained alternates are not counted as coverage because their main site was already trained or another alternate already covered it.

Incomplete training. A participant trained in only one of the online and face-to-face components. Main sites in this group are counted as neither trained.

Averages. Means use every valid score for that assessment, so the number of records can differ by assessment. Records marked not found or no score are excluded.

Paired t-test. Two-tailed, pretest versus posttest, using only records with both scores. Not reported when a region has fewer than 5 paired records. Cohen's dz is the mean gain divided by the standard deviation of the gains.

Hake's normalized gain. g = (mean posttest − mean pretest) / (100 − mean pretest), computed on group means. Common reading: g < 0.3 low, 0.3 to < 0.7 medium, ≥ 0.7 high. Because pretest scores are already high, small absolute gains can still give a high g.

Zero pretests. 16 pretest scores of exactly 0% were treated as not taken and left out of every pretest average, t-test and gain calculation, because a pretest of 0 paired with a near-perfect posttest would inflate the gain. Set ZERO_PRETEST_IS_MISSING = False in the build script to include them.
